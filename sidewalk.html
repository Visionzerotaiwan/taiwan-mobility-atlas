"Is there a sidewalk" and "can a wheelchair, stroller, or visually-impaired pedestrian actually cross safely" are two different questions.

04 — 資料說明04 — Data Notes

資料來源：交通部「全國人行道整體區位」開放資料，兩次全國普查快照：2023 年 6 月（83,301 個路段，全國 10,167 公里）、2026 年 6 月（92,326 個路段，全國 10,939 公里）。逐路段欄位包含道路名稱、路段長度、人行道寬度、淨寬（扣除路燈、招牌等障礙物後的可通行寬度）、路緣斜坡設置數量、所在縣市與鄉鎮市區。Source: MOTC's national sidewalk-location open dataset, two nationwide snapshots — June 2023 (83,301 segments, 10,167 km total) and June 2026 (92,326 segments, 10,939 km total). Per-segment fields include road name, segment length, sidewalk width, clear width (net of obstructions such as poles and signage), curb-ramp count, and county/district.

路緣斜坡缺口率怎麼算：原始欄位 SW_RAMP 是「已設置之路緣斜坡數量」，但若此處本來就不需要斜坡（例如路段中段、無側向通行需求），會標記為「N」（2023）或留空（2026）。我們只在「需要斜坡」的路段中，計算 SW_RAMP 為 0（該裝而未裝）的比例，作為缺口率：這是一個保守、只算「真正缺口」的指標。How the ramp gap rate is computed: the source field SW_RAMP counts installed curb ramps, but is marked "N" (2023) or left blank (2026) where no ramp is needed at all. We compute the gap rate only among segments that DO need a ramp, as the share where SW_RAMP = 0 (needed but not installed) — a conservative measure of genuine gaps only.

已知限制：2023 年原始資料為 TWD97 二度分帶座標系統（EPSG:3826），2026 年官方已提供 WGS84 版本；本頁的行政區面量圖僅使用逐路段的「縣市／鄉鎮市區」欄位聚合統計，未使用逐路段座標，故不受座標系統差異影響。高雄市那瑪夏區與連江縣東引鄉的人行道資料存在於原始資料中，但因本站既有行政區界圖資（districts.geojson）缺少這兩區的邊界多邊形（與车禍地圖的既有限制相同），故無法在面量圖上著色，僅計入縣市總計與排行榜。嘉義縣資料中有 2 筆路段的縣市欄位誤植（應屬嘉義市西區），已於彙整時更正；苗栗縣「頭份市」與彰化縣「員林市」在原始資料中仍使用 2015 年改制前的舊名（頭份鎮／員林鎮），已於彙整時對應至現行名稱。Known limitations: the 2023 source used the TWD97 TM2 coordinate system (EPSG:3826); the 2026 release already ships a WGS84 version. This page's choropleth aggregates only the per-segment county/district fields, never per-segment coordinates, so it is unaffected by the coordinate-system difference. Sidewalk records exist for 那瑪夏 (Kaohsiung) and 東引鄉 (Lienchiang), but this repo's district-boundary file (districts.geojson) lacks polygons for these two districts (the same pre-existing gap as the crash maps), so they can't be shaded on the map — they're still counted in county totals and the ranking. Two segments in the Chiayi County file were mistagged (they belong to Chiayi City's West District) and were corrected during aggregation; 頭份/員林 (upgraded from 鎮 to 市 in 2015) are mapped to their current names.
